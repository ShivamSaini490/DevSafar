## Where visitors usually spend their time

Visitor attention in Puducherry clusters around three areas: the seafront promenade,
the French Quarter (often called White Town) with its colonial-era streets, and the
spiritual institutions linked to Sri Aurobindo. These areas are close to each other,
so a single short visit can usually combine them without much travel time.

## Sri Aurobindo Ashram

The Ashram is a spiritual community founded by Sri Aurobindo and Mirra Alfassa
(known as The Mother). It is associated with yoga and meditation and is one of the
best known institutions in the town. Check current visiting arrangements locally,
as timings can change.

## Auroville

Auroville is an experimental township founded in 1968 by Mirra Alfassa. Its central
landmark is the Matrimandir, a gold-coloured meditation structure. Auroville lies
outside the town centre; the Incredible India page describes it as roughly a
30-minute drive from Puducherry. Confirm access and any booking requirements for
the Matrimandir before travelling.

## Planning tip

Shops and small attractions often keep their own hours, so confirm timings on the
day of the visit.
